## Proposed questions

1. GPT-2 Medium reaches only 0.28 validation IIA and plateaus at train IIA
   0.53. Which check comes first: a rank sweep (e.g. 1, 2, 4, 8 at layers
   12–16), a learning-rate / training-budget change, or more training data?
   Should those settings stay identical across model sizes, or be tuned per
   model on validation?
   Caveat for any rank sweep: Makelov, Lange, and Nanda (2023), "Is This the
   Subspace You Are Looking for? An Interpretability Illusion for Subspace
   Activation Patching", show that a patched subspace can change the output
   without being the one the model uses, even with a single direction. A
   higher rank gives the optimizer more freedom to find such a subspace.
   Read every rank against the untrained-alignment baseline and on
   validation and test, not train. These checks catch overfitting but do not
   rule out the illusion; the paper proposes further checks.
2. Larger data: more data from the same distribution first, or generalization
   (unseen names or templates) first?
3. Should layers 7–9 also be tested at other token positions (N-1, N-2, name
   tokens)?
4. Is a 200-pair in-distribution validation set enough?
5. data-scale budget: equal optimizer updates (equal compute) or equal epochs
   (each example seen equally often) across 200 / 1000 / 5000 pairs?
6. Results from different GPU types (A30 vs RTX 2080 Ti) agree in every IIA
   count but not bit for bit. May they be compared directly, or should runs
   that are compared share one GPU type?
